## 📦 模块 capybara：心理状态、处事态度与性格特征 (Attitudes & Traits)

### 1. 警惕、坚capybara定、豁达与情绪隐忍 (Vigilance, Resilience & Unfazed States)

- **vigilant / vigilantly** -> 警惕的 / 警觉地 (保持高度清醒与戒备，随时防范潜在危险或盗贼)
    
  - _Example:_ The vigilant security guard noticed the stranger loitering near the school gates and immediately reported him.
    
- **warily** -> 谨慎地 / 警惕地 (带着极强的防备心理、小心翼翼地注视或接近某人或某物)
    
    - _Example:_ Remembering how the stray dog had barked at him yesterday, Caleb approached the alleyway warily.
    
- **unfazed** -> 泰然自若的 / 不受动摇的 (在面对突发变故、批评或灾难时，情绪完全不受干扰)
    
    - _Example:_ Despite the sudden blackout during his presentation, Shaun remained unfazed and continued speaking.
    
- **unwavering** -> 坚定不移的 (意志、信念或目光极其坚定，在重重困难面前绝不动摇)
    
    - _Example:_ The coach praised the team for their unwavering determination to win, even when they were trailing.
    
- **tenacious** -> 坚韧不拔的 (紧抓不放、百折不挠，形容在追求目标或克服危机时极具韧性)
    
    - _Example:_ Through tenacious research and months of hard work, the young scientist finally discovered a cure.
    
- **tactful / tactfully** -> 委婉的 / 极其得体的 (说话、做事非常讲究分寸和技巧，绝不会让对方感到尴尬或冒犯)
    
    - _Example:_ Instead of bluntly telling Megan that her singing was terrible, the tactful teacher suggested she practice more.
    
- **sit on the fence** -> 坐山观虎斗 / 持中立态度 (在双方争论或选举中两边不讨好、迟迟不表态)
    
    - _Example:_ When his two best friends were arguing over which game to play, Keith sat on the fence to avoid conflict.
    
- **aplomb** -> 沉着 / 泰然自若 (在极具压力的公开场合或考试中，展现出高度的自信与冷静)
    
    - _Example:_ The young pianist handled the difficult solo during the National Day Concert with remarkable aplomb.
    
- **composure / composed** -> 镇静 / 沉着的 (成功控制住慌乱或愤怒的情绪，恢复到面部毫无波澜的状态)
    
    - _Example:_ It took all of Mrs Tan's composure to remain calm when the mischievous student accidentally spilled ink.
    
- **earnest / earnestly** -> 诚挚的 / 认真地 (态度极其严肃、真诚，绝非开玩笑或敷衍了事)
    
    - _Example:_ The boy earnestly apologised to his neighbour for shattering the window pane while playing soccer.
    
- **studiously** -> 刻苦地 / 故意地 (不仅指极其用功地学习，还常考“故意、费尽心思地”避开某事)
    
    - _Example:_ Throughout the heavy scolding from his father, we noticed that Julian studiously avoided making any eye contact.
    
- **unabashedly** -> 大言不惭地 / 不以为耻地 (做令人尴尬或受指责的事却毫不感到羞愧或局促)
    
    - _Example:_ Even though he was caught cheating in the friendly board game, Ken unabashedly laughed it off.
    

### 2. 傲慢、虚荣、盲目与消极傲慢 (Arrogance, Vanity & Complacency)

- **arrogant / arrogantly** -> 傲慢的 / 傲慢地 (自高自大，瞧不起他人，总觉得自己高人一等)
    
    - _Example:_ The arrogant champion refused to shake hands with his opponent after the match.
    
- **conceited** -> 自负的 / 虚荣心强的 (对自己的外貌、才能或财富极度自我陶醉)
    
    - _Example:_ Ever since he won the talent show, Marcus has become so conceited that he stops listening to feedback.
    
- **complacent** -> 盲目乐观的 / 固步自封的 (满足于现状，丧失警惕，极易在随后的考试或比赛中翻车)
    
    - _Example:_ Winning the mid-year examinations made Lucas complacent, leading to his poor results in the finals.
    
- **imperious** -> 专横的 / 颐指气使的 (像帝王一样傲慢，强迫命令别人顺从自己)
    
    - _Example:_ The captain's imperious manner alienated his teammates, who grew tired of being ordered around.
    
- **pretentious** -> 矫情虚伪的 / 自命不凡的 (刻意装作很有文化、很有钱或很有社会地位)
    
    - _Example:_ The critic dismissed the movie as pretentious, claiming it had no real story or depth.
    
- **flaunt** -> 炫耀 / 招摇 (故意在人前展示自己的财富、才华或新衣服以赢取羡慕)
    
    - _Example:_ Rather than being humble, the lottery winner chose to flaunt his newfound wealth by driving a sports car.
    
- **boast** -> 自夸 / 吹嘘 (用言语向别人夸耀自己的成就或拥有的东西)
    
    - _Example:_ Nathan loves to boast about his perfect score in mathematics to anyone who will listen.
    

### 3. 焦虑、恐惧、慌乱与不知所措 (Anxiety, Panic & Bewilderment)

- **anxious** -> 焦虑的 / 挂念的 (因前途未卜、考试即将来临而感到心神不宁、极度紧张)
    
    - _Example:_ Primary 6 students often feel anxious as the national examinations approach.
    
- **bewildered / bewilderment** -> 困惑不解的 / 极为迷茫 (面对复杂局面、魔术或突发问题时大脑一片空白，完全理不清头绪)
    
    - _Example:_ The tourists looked bewildered by the complicated train transit map in the bustling city.
    
- **dazed** -> 神志恍惚的 / 懵了的 (因头部受到撞击、听到噩耗或极度震惊而无法清醒思考)
    
    - _Example:_ Following the violent collision, the driver stepped out of the car looking completely dazed.
    
- **distraught** -> 心如刀割的 / 极度焦虑的 (因失去亲人、遭遇重大惨剧而陷入精神崩溃、痛不欲生的状态)
    
    - _Example:_ The distraught mother wept bitterly when her toddler went missing at the crowded night market.
    
- **disoriented** -> 迷失方向的 / 晕头转向的 (找不到北，不清楚自己身在何处，或者时空感错乱)
    
    - _Example:_ Emerging from the dark cave into the bright sunlight, James felt temporarily disoriented.
    
- **paranoid** -> 患得患失的 / 疑神疑鬼的 (总觉得周围所有人都在密谋害自己、针对自己)
    
    - _Example:_ After reading a series of horror stories, Sharon became paranoid about walking home alone.
    
- **queasy** -> 恶心的 / 焦虑不安的 (不仅指胃里翻江倒海想吐，也指因紧张而感到心里发慌)
    
    - _Example:_ The turbulent flight made many passengers feel queasy and uncomfortable.
    
- **stumped** -> 被难倒的 / 无计可施的 (遇到极难的词汇题或数学题，彻底卡壳，想不出答案)
    
    - _Example:_ Even the top student in our class was completely stumped by the final bonus question.
    
- **hysteria / hysterically** -> 歇斯底里 / 歇斯底里地 (情绪完全失控，引发大喊大叫、大哭大笑的疯狂状态)
    
    - _Example:_ The fans screamed hysterically when their favourite pop idol stepped off the plane.
    
- **jump out of my skin** -> 吓得魂飞魄散 (被突如其来的巨响、拍肩膀或惊吓吓得整个人弹起来)
    
    - _Example:_ When the lightning struck right outside my bedroom window, I nearly jumped out of my skin.

## 📦 模块 2：社交互动、言语表达与人际冲突 (Social Interactions & Speech)

### 1. 责备、嘲讽、挑衅与敌意 (Scolding, Defiance & Hostility)

- **accusation / accusingly** -> 控诉 / 谴责地 (用言语或眼神严厉指责、判定某人做了错事)
    
    - _Example:_ She looked accusingly at her brother when she found her diary opened and read.
    
- **admonish** -> 警告 / 训诫 (长辈或老师对学生微小过错进行严肃、善意的规劝与告诫)
    
    - _Example:_ The librarian had to admonish the rowdy boys for making too much noise in the silent study zone.
    
- **berates** -> 痛斥 / 严厉责骂 (狂风暴雨般地指责、怒骂某人的错误行为)
    
    - _Example:_ The furious chef berates his kitchen assistants whenever a dish is returned by a customer.
    
- **belittle / belittling** -> 贬低 / 瞧不起 (用刻薄的言语把别人的努力、成就或梦想说得一文不值)
    
    - _Example:_ A true leader encourages his teammates instead of choosing to belittle their ideas.
    
- **criticised** -> 批评 / 挑剔 (指出计划、作业或行为中的缺点与错误)
    
    - _Example:_ The headmaster criticised the messy layout of the school newsletter and demanded a reprint.
    
- **defiant** -> 公然违抗的 / 顶撞的 (拒不服从管教，带着轻蔑与挑衅的态度公然对抗权威)
    
    - _Example:_ The defiant teenager crossed his arms and refused to apologize for his disruptive behaviour.
    
- **contempt** -> 蔑视 / 鄙视 (认为某人或某规则极其低劣、卑微，根本不屑一顾)
    
    - _Example:_ Cheating in an examination is a sign of clear contempt for the school's code of honour.
    
- **derogatory** -> 贬损的 / 侮辱性的 (带有强烈的贬义、毁坏他人名誉或人格的言语)
    
    - _Example:_ The referee issued a red card to the player for making derogatory remarks during the match.
    
- **insolence** -> 蛮横无礼 / 傲慢无礼 (晚辈对长辈、下属对上司表现出极度令人难以容忍的粗暴、无礼)
    
    - _Example:_ The student's persistent insolence towards the teacher resulted in an immediate suspension.
    
- **hostile** -> 敌对的 / 不友善的 (展现出强烈的攻击性、不合作态度或充满敌意的眼神)
    
    - _Example:_ The stray cat was highly hostile, hissing fiercely at anyone who tried to feed it.
    
- **provocation** / **provoked** -> 挑衅 / 被激怒 (故意制造事端以引发冲突；或被别人的无礼举动点燃怒火)
    
    - _Example:_ Joshua was provoked into a fight after the bully repeatedly insulted his family members.
    

### 2. 得体、赞赏、赞同与和解 (Commendation, Tact & Concord)

- **acknowledge** -> 承认 / 感谢 (公开承认某项事实的真实性，或对别人的付出表示感谢与致敬)
    
    - _Example:_ The headmistress stepped onto the stage to acknowledge the hard work of the recycling committee.
    
- **affirm** -> 坚定地肯定 / 证实 (郑重其事地向大众声明、确认某项事实或信念)
    
    - _Example:_ The sports club held a meeting to affirm their commitment to fair play and drug-free competitions.
    
- **amicably** -> 和睦地 / 友善地 (心平气和地解决争端，没有引发任何争吵或怨恨)
    
    - _Example:_ Although they disagreed on the pricing, the two businessmen settled the dispute amicably.
    
- **commended** -> 表扬 / 称赞 (官方、长辈或公开场合对某人的高尚品德或杰出表现给予正式高度赞誉)
    
    - _Example:_ The police inspector commended the brave bystander for chasing down the snatch thief.
    
- **congratulated** -> 祝贺 / 道喜 (在别人获得成功、获奖、生日或毕业时表达喜悦与祝福)
    
    - _Example:_ Family members gathered at the foyer and congratulated Sarah on her stellar examination results.
    
- **compliment** / **complimented** -> 赞美 / 夸奖 (称赞别人的外貌、口才、穿搭或优秀的工作表现)
    
    - _Example:_ The guests did not hesitate to compliment the chef on the scrumptious five-course dinner.
    
- **consensus** -> 意见一致 / 共识 (团队成员经过深入讨论后，最终达成各方都能接受的统一结论)
    
    - _Example:_ After brainstorming for an hour, the project group finally reached a consensus on their topic.
    
- **consent** -> 同意 / 许可 (正式允许某事发生，或批准别人的请求)
    
    - _Example:_ You must obtain your parents' signed consent before you can participate in the overseas camp.
    
- **persuaded** / **persuasiveness** -> 说服 / 说服力 (用强大的逻辑、证据或动人的言辞成功让别人改变主意)
    
    - _Example:_ The smooth-talking salesman successfully persuaded my grandmother to buy the expensive vacuum.
    
- **placated** -> 安抚 / 平息怒火 (通过道歉、让步或送礼物，让极其愤怒或委屈的人冷静下来)
    
    - _Example:_ The customer service agent placated the irate passenger by offering a full refund and free vouchers.
    
- **reconcile** -> 调和 / 握手言和 (让两个关系彻底闹僵的朋友重归于好，或者消除两种矛盾观点的冲突)
    
    - _Example:_ It is often difficult to reconcile these two opposing scientific theories without further research.

## 📦 模块 3：短语动词全考点矩阵 (The Ultimate Phrasal Verbs Matrix)

### 1. Back 系列 (撤退、违约与支持)

- **back away** -> 步步后退 (因恐惧、害怕眼前的人、危险动物或火灾而往后退)
    
    - _Example:_ The hiker backed away slowly when he saw a wild boar emerging from the bushes.
    
- **back down** -> 认输 / 妥协 / 放弃立场的 (在争论、抗议或强大的压力面前低头认错，不再坚持)
    
    - _Example:_ Neither of the two angry drivers backed down, causing a massive traffic jam along the road.
    
- **back out** -> 临时退出 / 违约 (在最后一刻由于反悔或无法兑现承诺而拒绝参加原定计划)
    
    - _Example:_ Tom had agreed to join the charity run, but he backed out at the last minute due to an ankle injury.
    
- **backed into** -> 倒车撞上 / 误打误撞陷入 (汽车向后倒退时撞到障碍物，或者形容人糊里糊涂卷入某事)
    
    - _Example:_ The distracted driver backed into a concrete pillar while trying to park his saloon car.
    
- **backed off** -> 停止干涉 / 退后 (退让、别再逼迫对方，或命令某人不要再插手干预自己)
    
    - _Example:_ The bully backed off immediately when the discipline master walked into the classroom.
    
- **backed up / backup** -> 倒车 / 证实支持 / 后备支援 (车辆向后倒车；或拿出证据支持某人的说辞；或电脑数据备份)
    
    - _Example:_ The manager's claims were backed up by the receipts and eyewitness accounts presented at the trial.
    

### 2. Bring / Brought 系列 (引发、抚养与执行)

- **bring about**  -> 导致 / 引发 (成为某件大事、改变、危机或灾难发生的直接导火索)
    
    - _Example:_ The new recycling initiative brought about a significant reduction in school waste.
    
- **bring along** -> 随身携带 (带上某人或某件有用的物品一同前往目的地)
    
    - _Example:_ Do remember to bring along your water bottles and writing materials for the field trip tomorrow.
    
- **bring in** -> 引入 / 引进 / 带来收入 (请专家来帮忙解决问题，或者引入新法律、赚取丰厚利润)
    
    - _Example:_ The popular cafe brought in a famous pastry chef from Paris to design their new dessert menu.
    
- **bring off** -> 艰难成功 (在极其困难、所有人都不看好的情况下，破釜沉舟地成功完成了某项艰难任务)
    
    - _Example:_ Against all odds, the young chess player brought off a stunning victory over the grandmaster.
    
- **bring out** -> 推出新品 / 激发出潜能 (出版新书、上市新产品，或者让某人的某种性格品质展现出来)
    
    - _Example:_ The sports carnival aims to bring out the competitive spirit and teamwork within every student.
    
- **bring up**  -> 抚养长大 / 提及话题 (从小把孩子养育成人，或者在会议或聊天中突然提出某话题)
    
    - _Example:_ The teacher reminded Julian not to bring up that sensitive topic during the class discussion.
    

### 3. Call / Called 系列 (取消、拜访与呼吁)

- **call after** -> 紧随其后叫喊 (在某人走远或离开时，大声呼喊他们的名字试图让他们停下)
    
    - _Example:_ Seeing that his brother had left his lunchbox on the shoe rack, Aaron ran to the door to call after him.
    
- **call for** -> 需要 / 呼吁 / 值得 (形色严峻需要某种处理方式，或是公开号召大众做某事，亦或是值得庆祝)
    
    - _Example:_ The Principal's promotion calls for a celebration, so the staff arranged a surprise party.
    
- **call in** -> 召集 / 请来协助 (打电话向警察、医生、修理工求助并请他们来到现场解决危机)
    
    - _Example:_ The manager decided to call in a technician when the main server suddenly broke down.
    
- **call on** / **call upon** -> 拜访 / 号召 / 邀请发言 (正式登门拜访某人，或公开号召、邀请某人在台上发表讲话)
    
    - _Example:_ The host called on the guest speaker to deliver the opening address for the seminar.
    
- **call out**  -> 大声呼喊 / 揭发指出错误 (大声叫出某人的名字，或是公开指出、抨击某人的虚伪或错误行为)
    
    - _Example:_ The teacher called out the names of the students who had forgotten to hand in their homework.
    
- **call over** -> 叫过来 (招手或大喊让原本站在远处的某人走到自己身边来)
    
    - _Example:_ The mother called her children over to the kitchen counter to taste the freshly baked cookies.
    
- **call off** -> 取消活动 (因天降大雨、恶劣天气或突发紧急状况而彻底取消原定的比赛、演出或会议)
    
    - _Example:_ The highly anticipated sports meet was called off due to the heavy downpour and lightning risk.
    
- **call up** -> 征召入伍 / 打电话联系 (打电话联系某人，或者国家正式征召某人加入国家队、服兵役)
    
    - _Example:_ During the holidays, Singaporean youths are called up to serve their National Service commitments.
    

### 4. Cut 系列 (削减、中断与分割)

- **cut back** / **cut down** -> 削减 / 减少用量 (减少开支、少吃甜食以减肥，或者砍伐树木)
    
    - _Example:_ To save enough pocket money for the new bicycle, Ryan decided to cut back on his daily snacks.
    
- **cut off** -> 切断 / 孤立 / 打断说话 (切断水源电源，使某地与世隔绝，或者在电话中突然断线、打断别人发言)
    
    - _Example:_ The sudden heavy landslide cut off the remote village from the rest of the mainland.
    
- **cut out** -> 剪下 / 删去 / 停止运作 (用剪刀剪下图案，删除不必要的段落，或者引擎突然熄火停止工作)
    
    - _Example:_ You should cut out sugary carbonated drinks from your diet if you want to improve your stamina.
    
- **cut up** -> 切碎 / 伤心欲绝 (把肉或纸张切成碎块，或者形容某人因遭遇沉重打击而内心备受折磨)
    
    - _Example:_ The mother carefully cut up the steak into bite-sized pieces for her young toddler.
    

### 5. Fell 系列 (瓦解、落后与泡汤)

- **fell apart** -> 散架 / 关系破裂 / 精神崩溃 (物品四分五裂，计划彻底泡汤，或人因遭遇惨剧而情绪彻底垮掉)
    
    - _Example:_ The ancient book was so fragile that its pages fell apart the moment I picked it up.
    
- **fell behind** -> 落后 / 跟不上进度 (由于生病没上学、偷懒而导致成绩或跑步速度落后于大部队)
    
    - _Example:_ Having missed two weeks of lessons due to chickenpox, Cheryl fell behind in her studies.
    
- **fell down** -> 摔倒 / 存在致命缺陷 (人脚滑摔在地上，或者理论、计划在某个核心逻辑上站不住脚)
    
    - _Example:_ The toddler tripped over the thick door mat and fell down, bursting into tears immediately.
    
- **fell for** -> 上当受骗 / 迷恋上 (落入别人的圈套、轻信谎言，或者无可救药地爱上某人)
    
    - _Example:_ Don't feel bad; even adults fell for that convincing online scam during the flash sales.
    
- **fell in** -> 排队集合 / 塌陷 (士兵或制服团体迅速列队集合，或者房屋屋顶因年久失修而向内塌陷)
    
    - _Example:_ The National Cadet Corps members were ordered to fell in at the school courtyard by sunrise.
    
- **fell off** -> 跌落 / 数量骤减 (从自行车、马背或桌子上摔下来，或者销量、热度大幅度下滑)
    
    - _Example:_ The careless boy fell off his bicycle and scraped his knee against the rough gravel road.
    
- **fell out** -> 闹翻 / 掉落 (因意见不合与朋友彻底绝交翻脸，或者牙齿、头发成把掉落)
    
    - _Example:_ The two best friends fell out over a minor misunderstanding and stopped speaking to each other.
    
- **fell over** -> 被绊倒 / 倾倒 (人在走路时被地上的障碍物、石头狠狠绊了一下而往前栽倒)
    
    - _Example:_ The tall vase fell over and shattered into pieces when the cat jumped onto the mantlepiece.
    
- **fell through** -> 计划泡汤 (合同、旅游计划、商业交易因最后一刻出现变故而彻底无法落实)
    
    - _Example:_ Our plans to go camping fell through because of the sudden travel restrictions and haze.
    

### 6. Get 系列 (传达、生存与克服)

- **get across** -> 传达清楚 (成功将复杂的想法、意图或观点解释清楚，让听众完全明白)
    
    - _Example:_ The campaign leader used simple diagrams to get his message across to the young children.
    
- **get ahead** -> 出人头地 / 取得领先 (在学业、事业或竞争中超越他人，取得更大的功名成就)
    
    - _Example:_ To get ahead in his class, Julian spends his weekends doing extra practices and reading.
    
- **get away** -> 逃脱 / 离开度假 (成功摆脱警察、犯罪现场，或者抽身离开繁忙的工作去度假)
    
    - _Example:_ The bank robber managed to get away in a stolen vehicle before the police arrived.
    
- **get behind** -> 拖欠 / 支持 (学业或账单进度落后，或者站在某人身后给予全力支持)
    
    - _Example:_ The entire community decided to get behind the student's initiative to build a neighborhood library.
    
- **get by** -> 勉强糊口 / 勉强应付 (资金、资源或语言能力有限，但勉强足够支撑过活或应付过去)
    
    - _Example:_ Even though her command of Mandarin was basic, Sarah could get by during her short trip to Shanghai.
    
- **get on** -> 上车 / 相处融洽 / 进展 (登上巴士、地铁，或者与某人关系和谐，亦或指项目进展顺利)
    
    - _Example:_ How do you get on with your new classmates? I hope they are welcoming and kind.
    
- **get out / get out of hand** -> 逃离 / 局势失控 (传出秘密，或者事态、小孩子打闹突然变得无法收拾)
    
    - _Example:_ What started as a minor argument soon got out of hand and turned into a full-blown physical fight.
    
- **get over** -> 克服 / 从病痛或打击中痊愈 (从感冒、失败的阴影、亲人离世的悲痛中彻底恢复过来)
    
    - _Example:_ It took James a long time to get over the disappointment of losing the debate finals.
    
- **get through** -> 熬过难关 / 消耗完 / 拨通电话 (度过考试周，消耗掉大量物资，或者成功打通求助电话)
    
    - _Example:_ With the support of his caring teachers, Marcus managed to get through the stressful exam week.
    

### 7. Put 系列 (推迟、忍受与表达)

- **put ... off / put off** -> 推迟 / 让人倒胃口 (延期举办活动，或者某人的口臭、邋遢行为让人彻底失去兴趣)
    
    - _Example:_ Never put off till tomorrow what you can complete today; procrastination always leads to panic.
    
- **put across** -> 表达清楚 (极其清晰、有说服力地向大众阐述自己的方案、建议或观点)
    
    - _Example:_ She put her arguments across so eloquently that the panel voted unanimously in her favour.
    
- **put aside** -> 搁置 / 存钱 / 抛开分歧 (存下零花钱以备不时之需，或者为了大局暂时抛开个人恩怨)
    
    - _Example:_ The two rival political parties agreed to put aside their differences to help the flood victims.
    
- **put away** -> 物归原位 / 收拾好 / 关进监狱 (把玩具、衣服整齐放回原处，或者把凶残的犯人投进大牢)
    
    - _Example:_ The teacher instructed the Primary 6 students to put away their electronic devices before the test started.
    
- **put down** -> 放下 / 羞辱 / 安乐死 (放下手中物品，用刻薄言语当众羞辱人，或者给重病宠物做安乐死)
    
    - _Example:_ It is cruel and uncalled for to put down your classmates just because they made a mistake.
    
- **put in** -> 投入时间精力 / 递交申请 (在训练中付出巨大的汗水和时间，或者正式提交表格申请)
    
    - _Example:_ If you put in enough effort into your revisions, you will see a vast improvement in your grades.
    
- **put on** -> 穿戴 / 上演 / 虚张声势 (穿上外套，上演话剧，或者故意装出某种腔调、语气)
    
    - _Example:_ The drama club members put on an incredible performance that left the audience in awe.
    
- **put up / put up with** -> 建造 / 提供住宿 / 忍受 (搭建帐篷，收留朋友借宿，或者强行忍受极其恶劣的噪音、脾气)
    
    - _Example:_ I cannot put up with your constant complaints and negative attitude any longer.
    

### 8. Take / Took 系列 (相似、接管与开始)

- **take after** -> 外貌或性格极其相似 (晚辈在长相、脾气、行为举止上极度像父母或家族长辈)
    
    - _Example:_ With his sharp mathematical mind and calm demeanor, Gabriel takes after his grandfather.
    
- **take in**  -> 吸收理解 / 收留 / 欺骗 (完全听懂复杂的课，收留流浪猫，或者被骗子的谎言所蒙蔽)
    
    - _Example:_ Many elderly citizens were taken in by the sweet promises made by the unscrupulous salesman.
    
- **take on**  -> 承担责任 / 挑战对手 (接下沉重的工作任务、职位，或者在比赛中正式对抗强敌)
    
    - _Example:_ After the manager resigned, Mrs Lim had to take on the responsibility of leading the department.
    
- **take to** -> 开始喜欢 / 形成习惯 (一见钟情般地迅速喜欢上某项新爱好、某人，或者养成某种习惯)
    
    - _Example:_ The stray kitten immediately took to its new owners, purring softly whenever they held it.
    
- **take up with**  -> 与...厮混 / 忙于某事 (整天和一群不务正业的人混在一起，或者时间精力被某事占满)
    
    - _Example:_ Ever since he joined the football team, all his weekends have been taken up with intensive matches.
    
- **take ... off / took off** -> 脱下 / 飞机起飞 / 突然大火 (脱掉衣服，飞机离地升空，或者某项生意、事业突然爆火)
    
    - _Example:_ The airplane took off safely from Changi Airport despite the turbulent monsoon weather.
    

### 9. Strike 系列 (袭击、删除与建立)

- **strike at** -> 袭击 / 直击痛点 (用力击打某人，或者某个突发行动或事实直击问题的核心、痛处)
    
    - _Example:_ The government's new policy aims to strike at the very root of low-income family challenges.
    
- **strike down** -> 推翻法律 / 因病倒下 (法院宣布某项法令无效，或者突如其来的大病让强壮的人卧床不起)
    
    - _Example:_ The prime minister was struck down by a severe bout of influenza right before the summit.
    
- **strike off** -> 除名 / 划掉 (从名单、医生名册、律师协会中彻底除名，或者用笔在纸上把错字划掉)
    
    - _Example:_ The errant doctor was struck off the medical register for violating professional safety codes.
    
- **strike out** -> 自立门户 / 划掉 / 失败 (离开父母独自闯荡，在棒球中被三振出局，或者在纸上划掉写错的内容)
    
    - _Example:_ Eager to see the world, the young graduate decided to strike out on his own and move abroad.
    
- **strike up** -> 建立交情 / 开始演奏 (主动走过去和陌生人搭讪攀谈起来，或者乐队突然开始演奏乐曲)
    
    - _Example:_ It is remarkably easy for extroverted children to strike up a conversation with anyone at the playground.

## 📦 模块 4：肢体动作、空间位移与感官形态 (Movements & Sensory Actions)

### 1. 步态、潜行与位移辨析 (Gait, Slinking & Displacements)

- **ambled** -> 漫步 / 溜达 (毫无目的地、悠闲自在且步履缓慢地散步，心情极度放松)
    
    - _Example:_ The tourists ambled through the quaint streets of the historical village, taking photographs.
    
- **trudged** -> 步履艰难地走 (由于极度疲惫、双腿酸痛、泥泞山路或心情沉重而深一脚浅一脚地吃力迈步)
    
    - _Example:_ Soaked to the skin, the exhausted soldiers trudged back to their camp through the thick mud.
    
- **slink** -> 潜行 / 偷偷溜走 (因做了亏心事、感到羞愧或不想被人看见，低着头猫着腰悄悄溜走)
    
    - _Example:_ Caught red-handed with the missing key, the mischievous boy tried to slink out of the room.
    
- **meander** -> 蜿蜒而行 / 闲逛 (河流九曲回肠地流淌，或者形容人毫无方向地在商场里瞎逛、在说话时跑题)
    
    - _Example:_ The narrow footpath continues to meander through the dense forest until it reaches the waterfall.
    
- **flailing** -> 手舞足蹈 / 胡乱抓踢 (手臂或双腿在空中毫无章法地剧烈晃动，多用于溺水挣扎、滑倒前的平衡保持)
    
    - _Example:_ Flailing his arms wildly in the water, the drowning boy screamed at the top of his lungs for help.
    
- **strutted** -> 昂首阔步 (高高抬起头、挺起胸膛傲慢地走着，炫耀自己的新衣服、美貌或大胜之后的骄傲)
    
    - _Example:_ The arrogant rooster strutted around the farmyard as if he owned the entire property.
    
- **spun** -> 迅速转身 / 旋转 (整个人由于震惊、被拍肩膀而猛地转过身来，或者物品飞速转圈)
    
    - _Example:_ The dancer spun around gracefully on her tiptoes, completely mesmerizing the audience.
    
- **swaying** -> 摇摆 / 动摇 (树枝在狂风中来回摇晃，或者形容人的意志、决定开始产生动摇)
    
    - _Example:_ The tall coconut trees were swaying violently as the powerful typhoon approached the coast.
    

### 2. 声音、触觉与微观状态 (Sounds & Micro-actions)

- **rustling** -> 沙沙作响 (风吹动落叶、树枝，或者有人在黑暗中翻动报纸、塑料袋发出的轻微、连续的声音)
    
    - _Example:_ The sudden rustling of dry leaves in the bushes made the cautious campers freeze in fear.
    
- **stench** -> 恶臭 (死老鼠、垃圾堆、腐烂食物或下水道散发出的极其难闻、令人作呕的刺鼻气味)
    
    - _Example:_ The unbearable stench from the choked sewage pipe forced the residents to seal their windows tightly.
    
- **scalded** -> 被烫伤 (皮肤由于不小心接触到滚烫的开水、热汤、蒸汽而红肿受伤)
    
    - _Example:_ The clumsy waiter scalded his hand when he accidentally spilled the piping hot soup.
    
- **charred** -> 烧焦的 / 焦黑的 (物品被熊熊大火无情吞噬后，地上一片漆黑、碳化的惨烈景象)
    
    - _Example:_ Nothing remained of the wooden cottage except for a few charred logs and ashes.
    
- **charity** / **clarity** -> 清晰 / 明朗 (考纲核心高频词，指说话逻辑极其清晰，或者水质清澈见底，视线毫无遮挡)
    
    - _Example:_ The teacher explained the complex mathematical formula with such clarity that everyone understood it.
    
- **misty / hazy** -> 雾气蒙蒙的 / 烟雾弥漫的 (由于清晨水汽、眼镜起雾感到模糊，或者由于印尼烧芭引发的烟霾导致能见度极低)
    
    - _Example:_ The driver had to slow down his vehicle because the mountain pass was incredibly misty.
    
- **shone** -> 发光 / 闪耀 (太阳、手电筒强烈地照亮黑暗，或者形容小孩子双眼里闪烁着兴奋、聪慧的光芒)
    
    - _Example:_ The torchlight shone brightly into the deep cavern, revealing a hidden path ahead.
    
- **dim** -> 昏暗的 / 模糊的 (灯光微弱看不清字，或者记忆由于年代久远已经开始变得残缺不全)
    
    - _Example:_ Reading under such dim light will inevitably strain your eyes and worsen your eyesight.

## 📦 模块 5：数量、程度、时间边界与极端分配 (Quantities, Degrees & Time)

### 1. 匮乏、昂贵与吝啬 (Scarcity, Exorbitance & Meager Allocations)

- **meagre** -> 极其微薄的 / 寒酸的 (数量或金额极少，仅够勉强果腹或维持最低限度生存，令人感到可怜)
    
    - _Example:_ The struggling family had to get by on a meagre supply of rice and canned food for the entire week.
    
- **scanty** -> 稀少的 / 不足的 (特指布料极少，或者收集到的数据、证据和信息非常残缺，完全不足以得出结论)
    
    - _Example:_ Due to the scanty evidence collected by the forensic team, the suspect had to be released.
    
- **scarce** -> 稀缺的 / 供不应求的 (由于自然灾害、特殊情况引发市面上某项生活必需品极度匮乏，千金难求)
    
    - _Example:_ Fresh water became incredibly scarce in the village after the single water pump broke down.
    
- **exorbitant** -> 价格高得离谱的 (漫天要价、严重超出合理范围，专指不良商家的剥削或极度高昂的收费)
    
    - _Example:_ The tourist flatly refused to pay the exorbitant price demanded by the dishonest taxi driver.
    
- **extravagant** -> 奢侈浪费的 / 挥霍的 (买一堆根本用不到的花哨物品，或者花光所有积蓄去办极其奢华的派对)
    
    - _Example:_ Spending thousands of dollars on a single party dress is considered highly extravagant by her parents.
    
- **covetous** -> 贪婪的 / 垂涎的 (对别人的财产、玩具、地位极度渴望并想据为己有，透露出病态的妒忌)
    
    - _Example:_ The greedy stepbrother cast a covetous glance at the expensive gold watch Marcus received.
    

### 2. 充沛、过剩与额外增补 (Abundance, Excess & Supplements)

- **abundant** -> 极其丰富的 / 资源充沛的 (形容农产品大丰收，或者森林、矿产资源充裕，完全不愁不够用)
    
    - _Example:_ The volcanic island is known for its abundant wildlife and lush rainforests.
    
- **bountiful** -> 慷慨慷慨的大丰收 (大自然恩赐的充足资源，或者慈善家极其慷慨大方赠予的大量资助与礼物)
    
    - _Example:_ The orphanage residents offered a prayer of thanks for the bountiful meal prepared by the volunteers.
    
- **superfluous** -> 多余的 / 累赘的 (现有的数量已经完全够用了，额外添加的部分成了画蛇添足、毫无必要的垃圾)
    
    - _Example:_ Your essay is well-written, but you should remove these superfluous words to keep it within the word limit.
    
- **supplement** -> 额外补充 (通过服用保健品、做兼职、或者加刷几套模拟卷，来填补原本的营养、收入或知识空缺)
    
    - _Example:_ Julian took on an evening job to supplement his main income and pay off his school fees.
    

### 3. 时间频率、瞬间与周期辨析 (Temporal Boundaries & Frequencies)

- **biannually** -> 一年发生两次 (在单一年份内，每隔半年就会雷打不动举办一次，最典型考点如年中和年终考试)
    
    - _Example:_ The school health inspection takes place biannually—once in January and once in July.
    
- **biennially** -> 两年发生一次 (每隔整整两年才会举办一次，常考于国际运动会、双年双画展或大型盛会)
    
    - _Example:_ The regional youth sports carnival is held biennially, so the next one will be two years from now.
    
- **perennially** -> 长年累月地 / 终年地 (形容某个老毛病、大水患、交通堵塞常年存在，无法根除)
    
    - _Example:_ The low-lying road outside the mall is perennially flooded during the year-end monsoon season.
    
- **instant** -> 瞬间的 / 立即的 (没有一分一秒的延迟，在极短的刹那内直接发生的动作或结果)
    
    - _Example:_ The magician's trick was an instant success, drawing loud cheers from the stunned crowd.
    
- **abrupt** -> 突然的 / 唐突的 (动作毫无征兆地戛然而止，或者说话态度极其生硬粗鲁让人难以接受)
    
    - _Example:_ The bus came to an abrupt halt when a stray dog suddenly darted onto the road.
    
- **ceased** -> 停止 / 终止 (彻底停下某个状态，最常考搭配为：雨停了，哭声止住了，或者工厂停产)
    
    - _Example:_ The heavy rain finally ceased by evening, allowing the stranded students to head home.

## 📦 模块 6：破坏、灾难、身体生理与环境变故 (Destruction, Health & States)

### 1. 灾难、瓦解与废墟 (Calamity & Ruin)

- **calamitous** -> 灾难性的 / 损失惨重的 (引发了巨大的经济损失、人员伤亡或彻底毁灭性的惨烈结局)
    
    - _Example:_ The sudden collapse of the major dam had calamitous consequences for the towns downstream.
    
- **demolished** -> 彻底拆毁 / 推平 (用推土机、炸药把老旧建筑、非法建筑夷为平地，使其彻底不复存在)
    
    - _Example:_ The old concrete stadium was demolished to make way for a modern community hub.
    
- **wrecked** -> 严重损毁的 / 破坏的 (汽车被撞成废铁，船只触礁沉没，或者形容计划、名誉被彻底搞砸)
    
    - _Example:_ The storm wrecked the wooden pier, leaving pieces of broken timber floating in the sea.
    
- **rubble** -> 瓦砾 / 建筑废墟 (房屋被大火烧毁、被地震震塌后，满地遗留下来的碎砖烂瓦、水泥土块)
    
    - _Example:_ Rescue workers searched tirelessly through the rubble of the building to look for trapped survivors.
    
- **vandalised** -> 故意公物破坏的 (无视法律，在公共墙壁上乱涂乱画，或者故意砸坏公园长椅、电话亭)
    
    - _Example:_ The school management was furious to find that the newly painted pavilion had been vandalised overnight.
    
- **deteriorated** -> 恶化 / 每况愈下 (病情急剧转入危险期，或者老建筑年久失修烂掉，亦或是天气情况越来越差)
    
    - _Example:_ The patient's condition deteriorated rapidly, forcing the doctors to transfer him to the intensive care unit.
    

### 2. 生理不适、衰弱与感官状态 (Ailments & Bodily Reactions)

- **debilitating** -> 使人虚弱的 / 摧残健康的 (极度严重的疾病，长期折磨人的身体，让人完全丧失行动或生活自理能力)
    
    - _Example:_ Arthritis can be a debilitating disease that makes even simple movements like walking painful.
    
- **fainting** -> 昏厥 / 晕过去 (由于低血糖、极度缺氧、过度中暑或者极度惊吓，突然眼前一黑失去知觉倒地)
    
    - _Example:_ Several students felt dizzy and close to fainting after standing under the scorching sun for an hour.
    
- **fatigue** -> 极度疲劳 / 劳累 (由于长期高强度加班、熬夜复习或者长跑导致的深度生理、心理力竭状态)
    
    - _Example:_ Overcome by fatigue after the grueling ten-kilometer marathon, Keith collapsed onto the grass field.
    
- **feeble** / **frail** -> 虚弱的 / 弱不禁风的 (形容生病的老人或初生的小动物骨瘦如柴、连说话、站立都摇摇晃晃)
    
    - _Example:_ The old lady's voice was so feeble that the nurse had to lean in closely to hear her.
    
- **dampened** -> 泼冷水 / 降低热情 (字面意思为弄湿；考纲核心考查引申义：大雨或坏消息让大家高涨的热情大打折扣)
    
    - _Example:_ The sudden downpour dampened the continuous high spirits of the soccer fans at the open stadium.
    
- **delectable** / **scrumptious** -> 珍馐美味的 / 极为可口的 (形容五星级酒店大厨做的甜点或妈妈煮的晚餐香气四溢、好吃到舌头融化)
    
    - _Example:_ The bakery is famous for its delectable chocolate tarts and fresh fruit pies.

## 📦 模块 7：行政命令、司法与微观认知行为 (Executive, Legal & Cognitive Actions)

### 1. 强制、豁免、宣判与法律条文 (Coercion, Exemption & Legal Outcomes)

- **coerced** -> 强迫 / 胁迫 (使用暴力、严厉惩罚的威胁或者敲诈，强行逼迫某人违背自身意愿去做违法或违心的事)
    - _Example:_ The timid boy claimed he was coerced by the school bullies into stealing the textbook.
    
- **acquitted** -> 宣判无罪 / 当庭释放 (法官、陪审团在看完所有核心证据后，正式宣告被告人清白无辜，当庭解除拘留)
    - _Example:_ Due to a complete lack of concrete evidence, the suspect was acquitted of all criminal charges.
    
- **condoned** -> 姑息 / 纵容 (对严重违规行为、作弊、霸凌睁一只眼闭一只眼，不予惩罚，听之任之)
    
    - _Example:_ The discipline master emphasized that rowdy behavior towards teachers would never be condoned in this school.
    
- **abolished** -> 废除 / 取消法律 (官方通过出台新规定，正式宣告旧的落后制度、不合理的法律条文彻底作废)
    
    - _Example:_ Slavery was officially abolished in the country after a long and hard-fought political campaign.
    
- **exempted** -> 免除 / 豁免 (获得特权许可，成为唯一一个不需要遵守繁琐规则、交税、参加体育测验的人)
    
    - _Example:_ Students with medical certificates from government hospitals were exempted from the grueling physical test.
    
- **compulsory** -> 强制性的 / 必修的 (校规、国家法律明文规定的死命令，所有人必须无条件执行，绝无任何商量余地)
    
    - _Example:_ Attendance at the morning National Day assembly is compulsory for all staff and students.
    
- **deported** -> 驱逐出境 (因为非法入境、签证过期或者严重犯罪，被移民局官方强制遣返回原籍国)
    
    - _Example:_ The foreigners who were caught working without valid permits were arrested and promptly deported.
    

### 2. 信息检索、整理、判定与转化 (Cognition, Classification & Deciphering)

- **decipher** -> 破译 / 辨认 (费尽九牛二虎之力，去辨认极为潦草的医生字迹、远古时期的密码或破损的古文)
    
    - _Example:_ It took the English teacher a long time to decipher the messy handwriting in Julian's essay.
    
- **ascertain** -> 查明 / 弄清事实 (通过去现场仔细勘察、询问目击证人，最终百分之百锁定并确认事情的真相)
    
    - _Example:_ The police inspector reviewed the security camera footage to ascertain the exact cause of the crash.
    
- **classify** -> 分门别类 / 归类 (根据大小、颜色、物种、年代等核心特征，把杂乱无章的图书或标本整齐整理好)
    
    - _Example:_ The librarian showed the student volunteers how to classify the storybooks by genre.
    
- **compilation** -> 选辑 / 汇编 (把分散在各处的金曲、优秀范文、搞笑短视频，精心裁剪、收集后做成一个大合集)
    
    - _Example:_ This storybook is a beautiful compilation of traditional fables from all around Southeast Asia.
    
- **concoct** -> 调制 / 编造 (把各种奇奇怪怪的原料混合调制成饮料；更常考引申义：为了逃避惩罚，瞎编乱造谎言或借口)
    
    - _Example:_ The lazy boy tried to concoct an elaborate excuse about a lost bus card to explain his lateness.

## 📦 模块 8：大合集——高频考纲成语与修辞惯用语 (The Idiomatic Catch-All)

- **falling on deaf ears** -> 充耳不闻 / 成了耳边风 (长辈或老师千叮咛万嘱咐的真诚劝告，被学生当成空气，完全不予理睬)
    
    - _Example:_ The mother's continuous warnings about playing online games until late at night were falling on deaf ears.
    
- **finger in the pie** -> 强行插手 / 染指分一杯羹 (形容某人极度爱管闲事，无论什么活动、利益分配，他都想插一脚)
    
    - _Example:_ Knowing how much Shawn loves authority, he is bound to have a finger in the pie for the graduation project.
    
- **dodging a bullet** -> 躲过一劫 / 幸免于难 (千钧一发之际，因为临时取消行程或换座位，极为幸运地避开了一场大灾难或大惩罚)
    
    - _Example:_ Skipping that meeting turned out to be dodging a bullet; the boss was furious and scolded everyone present.
    
- **hard nut to crack** -> 难啃的硬骨头 (形容极难攻克的世界级数学难题，或者油盐不进、死活不肯开口招供的顽固嫌疑人)
    
    - _Example:_ The final bonus question in the mathematics paper proved to be a highly hard nut to crack even for top students.
    
- **bite the bullet** -> 咬紧牙关硬着头皮上 (面对避无可避的拔牙、巨额手术费或者极其痛苦的责罚，鼓起勇气去面对现实)
    
    - _Example:_ Knowing that his cavity would only worsen, Caleb decided to bite the bullet and book a dentist appointment.
    
- **bite his tongue** -> 强忍住不发火 / 欲言又止 (在差点冲口而出说出伤人恶语、剧透或者秘密的一瞬间，强行憋回去)
    
    - _Example:_ Although he disagreed with his father's harsh comment, Lucas decided to bite his tongue to preserve peace.
    
- **rock the boat** -> 捣乱 / 破坏大局稳定 (在整个团队、班级气氛极其融洽、项目顺利时，突然挑事破坏现有的和谐)
    
    - _Example:_ Everything is going smoothly right now, so please do not rock the boat by complaining about the minor rules.
    
- **break the ice** -> 打破僵局 / 活跃气氛 (在素不相识的陌生人群、新班级开学第一天，通过做游戏或开玩笑让气氛活跃起来)
    
    - _Example:_ The camp instructor shared a funny personal anecdote to break the ice on the first morning.
    
- **break a leg** -> 祝你演出成功 (演员、辩手演话剧或上台演讲前，朋友们之间用来表达真诚祝福的特殊俗语)
    
    - _Example:_ "You have practiced this piano piece for months, Sarah. Go out there and break a leg!" whispered her mother.
    
- **reading between the lines** -> 读懂字里行间的潜台词 (不仅看表面文字，更去深刻体会对方没有明说的真实意图、委婉拒绝或真实情感)
    
    - _Example:_ Reading between the lines of his short letter, I realized that my friend was actually deeply homesick.
    
- **grasping at straws** -> 抓救命稻草 / 绝望中的徒劳 (在毫无胜算、证据全无或者落水濒死的绝境中，采取毫无逻辑、极其无望的挣扎)
    
    - _Example:_ Without any solid proof to back up his claims, the lawyer was clearly just grasping at straws during the cross-examination.
